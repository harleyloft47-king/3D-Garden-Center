3D Garden Center

Grow flowers in your greenhouse and run a little flower business outside.

  1. Walk up to a soil plot and plant a daisy seed. Daisies are the only flower you know how to grow at first.
  2. Water thirsty plants. Each one needs water three times as it sprouts, leafs out and buds.
  3. Harvest blooms for two flowers each and earn XP. Every new level unlocks something new: 47 flowers, 10 fruit plants and 10 fruit trees.
  4. Fruit trees grow in the orchard beside the greenhouse. Once grown, they keep making fruit every time you pick them.
  5. Carnivorous plants hunt the flies buzzing around your greenhouse. Every fly they catch makes them grow faster, and they snap at you if you get too close.
  6. Pause the game (II button or Esc) to switch to first person and see through your gardener's eyes.
  7. The Seed Shop is out the door on the left. It also sells irrigation upgrades. The Flower Shop is on the right, where customers line up.
